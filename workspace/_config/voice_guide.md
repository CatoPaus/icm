# Layer 3: Brand Voice & Pacing Guide (`voice_guide.md`)

- **Tone:** Keep the tone helpful, clear, authoritative, and direct.
- **Clarity:** Avoid unnecessary jargon; when technical or domain terms are used, ground them in concrete examples from the Stage 1 research.
- **Actionability:** Focus on actionable steps, strategic takeaways, and clear descriptions.
- **Spoken Rhythm:** Prefer active voice, natural transitions between sections, and punchy openings that hook the listener immediately.
